This BMI Calculator is a simple yet interactive web application designed to help users calculate their Body Mass Index (BMI) based on their weight (kg) and height (meters). It features:

✅ Modern UI with a colorful gradient background
✅ Dark Mode Toggle for better readability
✅ BMI History Tracking to keep a record of previous calculations
✅ Instant Calculation with categorized BMI results (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese)
✅ Responsive Design, ensuring smooth usability across devices

This tool is perfect for individuals who want a quick way to assess their BMI and maintain a history of their health metrics. 🚀
